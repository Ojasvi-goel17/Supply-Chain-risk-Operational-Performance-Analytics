Supply Chain Risk, Operations & Performance Analytics

📌 Project Overview

End-to-end analysis of 180K+ supply-chain records using Python, SQL Server, and Power BI to evaluate profitability, logistics performance, delivery risk, and financial exposure.

🎯 Objectives

Analyze sales and profitability across products, categories, markets, and customers.

Evaluate delivery performance and logistics risk.

Identify loss-making and high-sales, low-margin products.

Quantify orders and sales exposed to delivery risk.

Identify high-priority supply-chain risk areas.


🛠️ Tools & Technologies

Python (Pandas, Matplotlib) | SQL Server | Power BI | DAX

🔄 Workflow

Raw Data → Python Cleaning & EDA → SQL Server Analysis → Power BI & DAX → Business Insights

📊 Power BI Dashboard

1. Executive Overview — Sales, Profit, Markets, Categories & Customer Segments

2. Profitability & Product Intelligence — Margins, Discounts & High-Sales/Low-Margin Products

3. Delivery & Logistics Intelligence — Shipping Performance, Delays & Delivery Risk

4. Supply Chain Risk & Operations — Revenue at Risk, Losses, Operational Risk & Risk Matrix

🔍 Key Insights

$36.8M Sales | $4.0M Profit analyzed across 180K+ records.

54.83% delivery-risk exposure, affecting approximately 36K orders and $20.13M associated sales.

First Class: 95.32% delivery-risk rate; Second Class: highest average delay at 1.99 days.

Identified 5 high-sales, low-margin products requiring review.

Shop by Sport: 9.91% margin, 55.15% delivery risk, $721K sales exposure.

0–10% discounts: highest total profit at $1.88M; No Discount: highest margin at 13.09%.

💡 Business Recommendations

Prioritize high-risk shipping modes and categories, review high-sales/low-margin products, optimize discount strategies, and focus on markets with high financial exposure.

📈 Outcome

Built a management-oriented supply-chain intelligence solution connecting profitability, logistics performance, delivery risk, and financial exposure to support data-driven operational decisions.
